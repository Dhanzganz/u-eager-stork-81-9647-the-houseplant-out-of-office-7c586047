# The Houseplant Out-of-Office

A one-line out-of-office auto-reply written as if a houseplant sent it: a cactus that has survived three years of neglect and now treats your email with the same patience it was shown.

## Entry

> Out of office: I'm a cactus. You haven't watered me since 2023 and I'm still alive. Your email can wait.

`main.py` writes that line (104 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
